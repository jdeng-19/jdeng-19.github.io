History 📚

Hello! My name is Julia Deng, and I am a first year History major at the University of California Riverside, and I am currently working towards a Bachelors of Arts degree for my history major. I have had experience working in volunteer clubs such as Key Club at my old high school, Walnut High School. I also participated as a member in more school related clubs, such as Chess Club and the DnD Club, both of which taught me how to work with individuals different from me, and how to cooperate with team members towards the same shared goal. I earned an award for my language skills in high school, and can understand Chinese Mandarin, and write a little bit in Mandarin. I’ve also received positive feedback on my writing abilities from professors, TA’s, and high school teachers, such as on essays, research papers, projects, etc. I’ve also done some freelance creative writing in my spare time as a hobby. Most of this freelance creative writing work has come in the form of unpublished, original stories worked on in my spare time. If you would like to contact me to view my written work, please contact me at the email address linked to this resume website.

In addition, I've also taken courses in graphic design, and have experience working with programs such as Adobe Illustrate, Photoshop, Clip Studio Paint, and Canva. I’ve had experience using the Canva program for seven years, and I also have extensive experience working with Clip Studio Paint due to my digital illustration work. I’m a self-taught artist of 6 years, and have done some part-time freelance work in the past for business clients and family friends. If you would like to view my portfolio, or view any examples of my graphic design work, please contact me at the email address linked to this resume website. Some fun details about me are that some of my biggest writer and art inspirations are Studio Ghibli’s films and general artstyle, Rick Riordan’s Percy Jackson series, Suzanne Collins Hunger Game series, and Julia Armfield’s books, specifically her horror novel, Our Wives Under The Sea. My favorite of the Studio Ghibli films are Totoro, Ponyo, and Kiki’s Delivery Service. I love the whimsical nature of all those movies, as well as the more down to earth emotions that resonate through each of the films even with all of the fantastical elements added on to it. There are also some social media artists I really look up to, and follow online, such as sacredhyacinth on Youtube, WolfyTheWitch on both Youtube and Instagram, and velinxi on instagram and twitter.

A few disclaimers. I do not use any AI in my written or drawn works, nor do I believe in the act of using AI to generate writing, art, or otherwise to replace work which can be done more accurately or correctly by a human. I will not work with a company that permits the usage of AI in art or written works, so please, if you are a company that uses AI in your art or marketing campaigns, do not contact me.
